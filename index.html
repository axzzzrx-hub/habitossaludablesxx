YOUR DAILY GUIDE

Six habits worth building

Healthy living is not about perfection. It is about making useful choices consistently and adapting them to your needs.

01

Eat Well

A healthy diet includes a variety of fruits, vegetables, legumes, whole grains, nuts, and other nutritious foods.

Tip: Reduce excess salt, free sugars, saturated fats, and industrially produced trans fats.

Read the source ↗
02

Move More

Regular physical activity can improve fitness, mood, sleep, and overall health.

Tip: Adults should aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity physical activity per week.

Read the source ↗
03

Sleep Better

Good sleep helps your body recover and supports mental and physical well-being.

Tip: Create a relaxing bedtime routine and try to keep a consistent sleep schedule.

Learn more ↗
04

Drink Enough Water

Water is essential for many functions in the body. Choose water regularly and limit drinks with large amounts of added sugar.

Tip: Hydration needs vary with age, climate, activity level, and health.

Read the source ↗
05

Manage Stress

Healthy strategies include breathing exercises, physical activity, spending time with loved ones, and taking regular breaks.

Tip: Relaxation techniques do not replace professional help when stress significantly affects daily life.

Learn more ↗
06

Build Healthy Routines

Simple routines make healthy choices easier to repeat and can help you organize your day.

Tip: Start with one small habit, make it realistic, and build gradually.

See all sources ↗

REMEMBER

Progress, not perfection.

Choose habits that fit your life and make small improvements over time.
